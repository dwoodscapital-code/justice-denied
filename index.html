Justice Denied?
    Winner

      How to play
      • Players take turns answering a card.
      • Right answer: +5 points. Wrong answer: -3 points (your score never goes below 0).
      • Your piece moves around the board as your score grows. First to 30 points makes it HOME and wins.
      • Each player picks a level: Easy gets easy cards, Medium gets easy and medium, Advanced gets every card.
      • Yellow Talk It Over cards have no right answer and no points. Talk about it as a family, then pass the turn.
      • Play the computer (1 to 5 computer players), or pass one phone around for 2 to 6 people.
